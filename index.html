About

Senior frontend engineer with 20 years in the field, the last 11 of them on a single product — HomeStory, a white-label home-search and agent-matching platform now live for 15+ U.S. financial institutions including Pennymac, SoFi, Citizens Bank, Fifth Third, Capital One, Fidelity and LendingTree. I've personally shipped every partner edition's frontend, all at WCAG AA or AAA.

I work in React, Next.js and TypeScript at scale, on multi-tenant platforms where each partner is its own tenant on shared underlying code. I came into engineering from graphic design, which shapes how I work — I care about visual correctness and interaction detail, not just whether something compiles. Comfortable enough on the backend (Node, REST, SQL/Mongo) to ship full features end to end without blocking - something I currently practice on my own SaaS boilerplate, Athletix, alongside its mobile companion in React Native and Expo.
